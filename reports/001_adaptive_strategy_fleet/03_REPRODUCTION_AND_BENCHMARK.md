# 03 — Reproduction and synthetic benchmark (Phase 4)

Everything here was **executed**; raw per-seed JSON is in `bench/results/`, tables regenerated by `bench/analyze.py`
(`bench/results/main_tables.md`, `bench/results/sweep_tables.md`). Nothing below is estimated or copied from documentation.

## 1. What is benchmarked — and what is not

The reward is an abstract *information value* (Bernoulli(p_cell) = "this evidence increment was informative"). No P&L, no capital, no costs are modelled.
The benchmark ranks **behaviours of allocation rules under one synthetic scenario family**, not "libraries in general".

**Top 3 by Phase-2 order that could be executed: River, VW, MABWiser.** Optuna and SMPyBandits are NOT_RUN in the allocation benchmark (reasons in §7).
Reference rules written by me (labelled `ref_*`, **CUSTOM, not external candidates**) are included only to give the numbers a frame: `ref_roundrobin` (least-recently-pulled first),
`ref_ducb` (Discounted-UCB per [arXiv:0805.3415](https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.3415), ~25 lines), `ref_ducb_fresh` (same + force-revisit if a cell is un-pulled > 200 rounds).

## 2. Scenario (`bench/bench.py`, `make_env`, constants at top)

| Element | Implementation |
|---|---|
| Cells | 160 total: 40 **established**, 100 **new** (no history), 20 **late** (arrive at round 300 → dynamic arm set) |
| Established / stale evidence | 40 cells get 200 logged pulls each before round 0 (uniform logging) through each library's normal update path. Established cells are then naturally at risk of **stale evidence** if attention leaves them. |
| Promise | established p~U(0.2,0.7); new/late p~Beta(2,6); 12 hidden **gems** p~U(0.7,0.8) (8 among new, 4 among late) |
| Non-stationarity + degradation | at round 600: the 10 best established cells drop by 0.4 (floor 0.05) = **degradation**; the 10 lowest-p new cells jump to 0.85 = **improvement/regime change** |
| Data gap | rounds 800–899: 20 random cells (never among the improved ones) return **no evidence**; the policy is **not told** (no update) — models "missing evidence" |
| Attention | 1,200 rounds × 10 distinct cells per round (delayed, batched feedback); 20 seeds; env seed = policy seed = s |
| Oracle | knows true p and gaps; picks top-10 available cells each round |

Metrics: **coverage** (`cov≥1`, `cov≥10`), **starvation** (cells never pulled; `stale@end` = fraction with no pull in last 200 rounds; max inter-pull gap; time-to-first-pull of new cells; gems found ≥20 pulls),
**attention concentration** (Gini, share of pulls on top-10% cells = 16 cells, normalised entropy `H`) before/after the change, **adaptation** (share of pulls on the 10 improved / 10 degraded cells in windows after round 600; regret vs oracle),
**gap waste** (share of attention in the gap window spent on silent cells), **determinism**, **runtime / memory / dependency weight**.
`regret` = oracle expected reward − achieved expected reward, per round of 10 pulls (round-robin ≈ 4.5; lower is better; *low regret alone rewards concentration*).

Reading the table (INFERENCE unless tagged):

1. **Reward-greedy allocation starves (PROVEN).** The lowest-regret rows (River UCB-Mean 1.04, both Thompson variants ≈1.0, MABWiser-UCB1 3.1 aside) leave **20–24% of cells never touched (33–38 of 160) and 72–82% stale at the end**.
   For `river_ucb_mean` seed 0 the never-pulled set was the *established* cells (39 cells, ids 0–39 except one; OBSERVED debug run) — i.e. well-evidenced cells silently fall out of attention: "stale evidence" is the default outcome, not an edge case.
2. **New-cell cold start:** MABWiser-UCB1 **PROVEN** to starve new cells (expectation 0 for untrained arms → 139/160 never pulled, gems found 0%; raising `alpha` to 2 and 5 leaves 122 and 120 zero-pull cells, sweep). River UCB (unseen ⇒ +inf) and the Thompson rules reach new cells within ~3.5–4.5 rounds (median time-to-first-pull); ε-greedy-type rules (River-ε, MABWiser-ε, VW-ε) take ~94–101 rounds (they touch new cells only through random exploration, yet still reach ≈100% of cells eventually); VW-squarecb 16.8 (`results/main_tables.md`).
3. **Adaptation is poor for cumulative estimators (PROVEN):** 150–200 rounds after the change only 0–3% (MABWiser/TS/VW-ε) to 26% (River UCB-Mean, lucky exploration) of pulls reach the 10 improved cells; `mabwiser_ucb1` never reaches them (0.00) and keeps 58% of attention on the degraded cells.
   Cumulative Beta/mean estimators cannot "unlearn"; `river_eps_ew` (EWMean) reaches 45%.
4. **Forgetting alone is not enough (PROVEN):** `river_ucb_ew` has regret 4.51 (= round-robin 4.51) in the main run and `regret_all` 4.25–4.79 across 9 sweep settings (fading ∈ {0.9, 0.97, 0.99} × δ ∈ {0.1, 0.5, 1.0}), i.e. never better than uniform — because the exploration bonus uses cumulative counts, not effective sample size (source-level cause, 02 §1; INFERENCE that this is the whole explanation).
5. **Silent cells hijack attention when the policy only learns from observed evidence (PROVEN in this scenario):** during the gap window, forgetting/UCB-type rules spent **72–89%** of all attention on the 20 silent cells (`river_ucb_ew` 0.72, `ref_ducb@0.98` 0.89, `@0.995` 0.83) vs 8–17% for cumulative rules that rarely revisit them anyway.
   Mechanism (INFERENCE): a pull that returns nothing changes no state, so a high-index cell stays high-index and is pulled again — a feedback loop. This is the direct analogue of "missing evidence is not negative evidence", and **no reviewed library offers an "attempted, no evidence" channel** (River/MABWiser/VW only take rewards; NaN is rejected by MABWiser and *poisons* River UCB — 02).
6. **VW `squarecb` is the only external rule with zero starvation and healthy concentration (Gini 0.56)**, but at the highest cost (13 s/run) and the worst adaptation (keeps 46% on degraded cells at lr 0.05; regret 3.95). Its learning rate is a monotone knob between "uniform" and "greedy" (sweep, below) — i.e. tuning replaces starvation with slow adaptation.

## 4. Sensitivity sweeps (5 seeds each, except `ref_ducb*` 20 seeds) — **PROVEN**, `results/sweep_tables.md`

| Rule | Setting → outcome (zero / stale / impr(150-200) / gap / regret) |
|---|---|
| `ref_ducb` discount γ | 0.98 → 0 / 0 / 0.14 / 0.89 / 4.01 · 0.99 → 0 / 0 / 0.19 / 0.86 / 3.57 · 0.995 → 0 / 0 / 0.35 / 0.83 / 2.98 · **0.998 → 7.9 / 0.05 / 0.85 / 0.33 / 1.72** · 0.9995 → 37.3 / 0.67 / 0.67 / 0.13 / 1.00 |
| `ref_ducbfresh` γ=0.998 | **0 / 0 / 0.85 / 0.28 / 1.72** (staleness guard: same regret and adaptation as γ=0.998, starvation removed; `cov≥10` 0.87 vs 0.85) |
| `river_ucbew` (9 settings) | zero 10.6–21.4; regret_all 4.25–4.79; impr 0.06–0.24 → structural, not a tuning issue |
| `mabwiser_ucb1` α ∈ {0.5, 2, 5} | zero 139 / 122 / 120 → structural cold-start defect |
| `vw_squarecb` lr ∈ {0.005, 0.05, 0.5} | Gini(pre) 0.33 / 0.56 / 0.71; regret_all 4.26 / 3.95 / 3.35; zero-pull 0 for all; degr share (150-200) 0.12 / 0.46 / 0.50 |

Interpretation (INFERENCE): the D-UCB discount trades **coverage/starvation vs adaptation speed** along a monotone frontier; a **staleness-forced revisit** dominates the frontier point γ=0.998 in this scenario. This shows the *shape* of the design space, **not** that γ=0.998 or the 200-round threshold generalise (they were selected after seeing results on the same scenario ⇒ in-sample; UNKNOWN out-of-sample).
The `ref_ducb_fresh` guard never fires at γ=0.98 (max inter-pull gap ≈ 117 < 200), so `ref_ducb` and `ref_ducb_fresh` rows are identical there (OBSERVED equality in `main_tables.md`).

## 7. Not executed / limitations — stated explicitly

| Item | Status | Why |
|---|---|---|
| SMPyBandits SW-UCB/D-UCB/discounted TS | **NOT_RUN (execution impossible unpatched)** | `ImportError: btdtri` on SciPy 1.17.1 (PROVEN). I did not patch a stale dependency. `ref_ducb` reimplements the paper's algorithm instead; it is my code, not SMPyBandits'. |
| Optuna successive halving in the *allocation* benchmark | **NOT_RUN** | No API for "which cell gets the next pull" over a growing, drifting arm set; pruning is terminal. I only demonstrated determinism of a seeded SHA study (identical, 54 pruned/6 complete) and ran its own 125 tests. Whether SHA-style rungs help here is UNKNOWN. |
| River drift detectors (ADWIN etc.) as restart triggers | **NOT_RUN** | Out of time-box; effect on adaptation UNKNOWN. |
| VW feature sharing across cells / `epsilon_decay` champion-challenger | **NOT_RUN in benchmark** | Only per-cell-id features used ⇒ VW's main advantage untested. |
| Exp3 | **ERROR** | harness feeds history and delayed batches; Exp3's API cannot (02). |
| Statistical significance | not computed | mean±sd over 20 seeds only; sweeps use 5 seeds. |
| Single scenario family | limitation | Bernoulli rewards, stationary noise, one change-point, one gap; real cell dynamics UNKNOWN. |
| VW harness approximation | limitation | logged probability = probability at each sequential draw without replacement (not exact IPS for batch selection) — INFERENCE that this slightly biases VW's learning. |
| MABWiser harness | limitation | top-10 of `predict_expectations()` (for TS these are fresh samples), and one `partial_fit` per round; uses the library as shipped, no custom warm-start of new arms. |
| Runtime wall clock | caveat | 4 jobs in parallel on 4 cores. |
