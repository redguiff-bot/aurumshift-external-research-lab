# 13 — Limitations

1. **Synthetic only.** The simulator is my construction. Conclusions hold for its assumed opportunity mix, edge decay (e^(−age/8 h)), score model (linear in edge plus persistent and iid noise, per-instrument noise level), duration distributions and cost levels. Real edge decay, score quality, cost structure and duration are UNKNOWN. Nothing here is evidence for or against any live/paper behaviour, and nothing supports changing `max_open_positions`.
2. **No production cap recommendation.** Capacity levels 3/4/6/10 are sensitivity points; arrival intensity is anchored at C = 4, so C = 3 is "more saturated" only because of that anchor.
3. **FIFO's weakness partly comes from the model.** FIFO serves the stalest opportunity first while edge decays with waiting; part of the "FIFO failure" is that freshness effect (post-hoc `LIFO_NETPOS` diagnostic). If real candidates do not decay, or the pending horizon is shorter, the gap to FIFO shrinks. A FIFO with a net-positive filter (`FIFO_NETPOS`) is the fairer control for most claims and is reported next to every FIFO number.
4. **Statistical scope.** CIs are over seeds × 3 variants × 15 families; variants are draws from a jitter distribution, so uncertainty over scenario *design* is not captured. Blocks are treated as independent although variants share structure. 29 policies were compared; the shortlist (chosen mechanically on validation) limits but does not remove selection bias; the per-family best-of table (09) is optimistic by construction.
5. **Tuning.** 92 configurations over 17 tunable policies were tuned on three seeds of the tuning split. Several optima sit on grid edges (`SLOTHOUR_HAZARD τ`, `ONLINE_KNAPSACK_PSI U`, `CORR_PENALTY g`, `CORR_HARD_REJECT ρ`, `EVICT_SWAP` min-hold, `SLOTHOUR_DENSITY h0`, `LINUCB α`), meaning "knob off" or "grid too small"; grids were extended once. Bandit and expert hyper-parameters received a smaller search than the threshold methods; a better-tuned or differently-featured bandit was not excluded.
6. **Decision thresholds were set after seeing validation results** (on the corrected scale), and the pre-registration was rebuilt once (v1 → v2) before any held-out data existed. The v1 material is archived. The verdict margin is thin (0.023 vs bar 0.02): see 12 for the threshold sensitivity.
7. **Metric.** The primary metric is latent expected net per slot-hour (noise-free), chosen for statistical power; realised outcome (`real_*`) tracks it closely in the means (e.g. FIFO 0.158 vs 0.162) but individual runs are noisy. Normalisation by RMS opportunity density makes families comparable but is a judgement.
8. **Risk model.** Diversification uses a rolling estimated covariance (EWMA, window ≈ 100 h, shrink 0.05); Ledoit–Wolf was more accurate at long windows (11) and was not tried inside a policy. Risk metrics are daily PnL volatility and drawdown of a fixed-size position book; no position sizing, no leverage, no market impact, no partial fills, no fee tiers.
9. **Regime shifts.** Tested with permanent step changes (edge permutation, calibration flips). No tested method adapted within 500 h; faster-adapting estimators (windowing, change-point detectors, discounted learning) were not implemented.
10. **Spam/gaming model.** A single instrument with a constant score bias. Adaptive adversaries, identity-level de-duplication, and correlated spam across instruments were not tested.
11. **Oracles.** `ORACLE_SCORE`/`ORACLE_DENSITY` are greedy references, *not* upper bounds (threshold methods beat them). The hindsight LP is an upper bound for the modelled admission-time choices only (no early exit).
12. **Leakage tests** prove absence of lookahead only for the tested interface, scenarios, perturbations (latent-of-rejected, everything after t0 = 400) and 5 cases; they do not prove a future modification is safe. The static audit is a token search.
13. **Literature references** are recalled from memory (DOCUMENTED_CLAIM); they were not re-fetched, and no source-code inspection of external libraries beyond executing them was done. Library maintenance judgements use only PyPI release metadata and installed package metadata. Vowpal Wabbit, Riskfolio-Lib and Open Bandit Pipeline were **not executed**.
14. **Post-hoc analyses** (`LIFO_NETPOS`, `MARGINAL_DIAG`, the threshold-sensitivity paragraph) are exploratory, were run on validation worlds (or offline from held-out output for the sensitivity), and carry no verdict weight.
15. **Process disclosure.** The first partial state of this branch was merged as PR #6 by a repository actor while the study was in progress; work continued on the branch recreated from `main`. A container restart stopped one monitor while the validation run was finishing; the run's output was verified complete (16,704 rows, 576 cells × 29 policies) and used as is.
