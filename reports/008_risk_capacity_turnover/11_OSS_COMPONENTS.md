## Findings from executing the components
* **The Ledoit–Wolf cross-check exposed a real defect in my first covariance estimator** (a slow-decaying prior of `eye·40` and shrinkage 0.2 that biased correlations toward zero for hundreds of steps and was too strong for tightly clustered instruments). That found bug drove the superseded run 1 → run 2 change (04). Residual: the final EWMA is still noisier than LW at long windows.
* **ANY_DROP_IN_ALLOCATOR = no.** No library evaluated is a ready allocator for "admit scarce slots among time-stamped opportunities". SciPy/OR-Tools solve the *batch* selection exactly when needed; the online rules are a few dozen lines each and are better treated as ADAPT_CANDIDATE reference implementations (in `bench/capacity_v1/py/policies.py`) than as dependencies.
