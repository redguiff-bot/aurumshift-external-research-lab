# 012 — Uncertainty calibration & abstention: executive summary

Date: 2026-09-29 · Mode: EXTERNAL_RESEARCH_ONLY · No private AurumShift code was read or assumed · No integration claim is made.
Evidence tags follow the lab doctrine: **PROVEN** (mathematical/deterministic), **OBSERVED** (measured here), **DOCUMENTED_CLAIM** (literature, not reproduced), **INFERENCE**, **UNKNOWN**.

## What was done
* A controlled synthetic BUY/SELL/HOLD world (AR(1) features, clustered volatility, rare-cluster rows, exact oracle posteriors) with **8 scenarios** (none, volatility jump, regime transition, feature shift, missing features, stale features, venue change, rare cluster) plus an iid control; **12 held-out seeds at full shift magnitude**, **4 tuning seeds at 0.6× magnitude**; two base learners (well-specified logistic regression, deliberately over-fit gradient boosting).
* Public data: OpenML ELEC2 electricity (real, time-ordered, drifting; 33k held-out rows), credit-g and breast-cancer (iid re-splits, 20 reps), California housing (iid vs covariate-shift split).
* 52 methods catalogued, **33 executed** (`../../bench/uncertainty_v1/results/tables/methods_catalog.md`).
* Train / calibration / validation / held-out are separate; every hyper-choice was made on tuning seeds or validation and frozen in `bench/uncertainty_v1/frozen_config.json` before the held-out run of that component. A label-maturity delay (H=10) is enforced and unit-tested (`tests/test_no_lookahead.py`, 4 tests pass).

## Answers (all OBSERVED on synthetic + ELEC2 unless stated)
**Q1 — can confidence be made empirically calibrated?** Yes, *in the stationary regime*. For an over-confident learner temperature scaling cuts ECE 0.249 → 0.027 and confident-wrong mass (CWM, conf ≥ 0.6 and wrong) 0.336 → 0.073 (paired CIs exclude zero). For an already well-specified model calibration adds nothing (Δ within CI). One-parameter temperature scaling was as good as or better than beta/isotonic/BBQ-lite; Platt failed on saturated confidences (ECE 0.119).
**Q2 — abstention without collapsing opportunities?** Only weakly. At 25 % abstention on trades, confidence ranking removes 36 % of losing trades while keeping 83 % of winning ones (random: 25 % / 75 %) — a real but modest lift (~1.4×). But with this (toy) payoff even low-confidence trades have positive expected value, so utility-optimal abstention is only ~5 % and larger budgets *reduce* total utility (−3 % at 25 %, −13 % at 40 %). Abstention becomes clearly valuable only when trades turn negative-EV under shift *and* the confidence is recalibrated online (regime transition: total utility −151 → −36; stale features +122 → +146). Ensemble disagreement was **not** a better abstention score than max-probability. Conformal-singleton abstention (α=0.2) discards 58–76 % of decisions.
**Q3 — does calibration survive regime change?** **Static calibration does not** (temperature-scaled GBM ECE drift post−pre: feature shift +0.126, regime transition +0.150, stale +0.108, venue +0.066, volatility jump +0.053). **Online recalibration with matured labels largely restores it** (shift-only mean ECE 0.107 → 0.025; CWM 0.127 → 0.025) after a transient of ≈500–1000 steps, at no measurable cost in the stationary world. Same pattern on real ELEC2 (ECE 0.16 → 0.02–0.04; static recalibration even *hurt* the LR Brier, 0.456 → 0.478).
**Q4 — can uncertainty distinguish NO_SIGNAL / DATA_GAP / MODEL_UNCERTAINTY / OOD?** Partially. DATA_GAP is separable *only via explicit missing/repeat flags* (P=R=1.0 by construction; without those flags recall is 0, mean-imputed and stale rows look in-distribution). NO_SIGNAL vs NORMAL is separable from calibrated confidence (F1 0.71). OOD is detected with high precision (0.97) but low recall (0.39). **MODEL_UNCERTAINTY was not separable** (F1 0.07). Volatility jumps and regime transitions are invisible to input-space detectors (flag rate 5.9 % = false-alarm level while error rate is 49–64 %); only a realised-loss monitor on matured labels detects them (100 % of seeds, delay 150–500 steps).
**Conformal validity.** Split conformal is valid in the iid control (0.902 ± 0.006) and collapses under shift (mean coverage 0.766, target 0.90; 0.54 for a volatility jump; ELEC2 blocks 0.59–0.85 vs 0.80; California covariate shift 0.59). ACI / rolling / recency-weighted methods restore *long-run marginal* coverage (0.89–0.90) but not conditional coverage (low-vol 0.96–0.98 vs high-vol 0.82–0.84), can produce infinite intervals (ACI on split scores: 9 % of steps), undercover transiently (worst 300-step window 0.82–0.88), and their guarantee with delayed feedback is empirical here, not proven. No claim of finite-sample time-series coverage is made.

## Recommendation (external candidates, not an integration verdict)
* **BEST_SIMPLE_REFERENCE:** temperature scaling on the logits, fit on a held-out calibration split, plus a validation-chosen confidence threshold for abstention.
* **BEST_ONLINE_REFERENCE:** the same temperature scaling refit every 50 steps on a sliding window (W=600) of *matured* labels only (H=10), with a realised-loss monitor as the shift alarm; ACI-on-rolling-scores (γ=0.01) as a marginal-coverage monitor for intervals.
* Do not claim calibration under regime change without label-delay-safe online recalibration; do not use split-conformal coverage numbers under drift; do not expect abstention alone to raise utility.
